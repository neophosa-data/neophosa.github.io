Customer Churn Analysis

Analysing customer retention, churn behaviour, service interactions and customer segments using SQL and Power BI.

Business Problem

Customer churn reduces recurring revenue and increases the cost of acquiring new customers. Individual customer records can make it difficult to understand the overall scale of churn and identify patterns in customer behaviour.

The objective of this project was to understand the customer base, churn levels and retention patterns using SQL and Power BI.

The analysis provides management with a clearer view of customer retention and supports more targeted retention strategies.

Business Questions

The analysis focused on answering the following questions:

01

How many customers are in the dataset?

02

How many customers churned?

03

What is the overall churn rate?

04

How many customers were retained?

05

What patterns help understand customer retention?

Business Interpretation

The analysis showed an association between customer service interactions and churn, with customers making more service calls showing higher churn behaviour.

Area code 415 contained 2,592 customers, of which 352 customers churned.

Overall customer retention can be understood through service interactions, customer usage behaviour and geographic segments.

Recommendations

  • Investigate customer service experiences that may contribute to churn.
  • Monitor churn across different customer segments and geographic areas.
  • Identify higher-risk customers for targeted retention activities.
  • Monitor customer usage behaviour to identify changes associated with retention.
  • Build recurring churn reporting to monitor retention performance over time.
