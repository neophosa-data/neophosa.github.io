2. Customer Churn Analysis

Project Overview

This project analyses customer churn to identify factors contributing to customer attrition and provide recommendations for improving customer retention.

Business Problem

Customer churn directly impacts revenue and profitability. Understanding why customers leave enables businesses to develop effective retention strategies.

Objectives

  • Measure overall churn.
  • Identify regions with high churn.
  • Analyse customer behaviour.
  • Identify patterns associated with churn.
  • Recommend retention strategies.

Tools Used

  • Excel
  • SQL
  • Power BI
  • DAX

Dataset Overview

The dataset includes customer information such as:

  • State
  • Area Code
  • Account Length
  • International Plan
  • Voice Mail Plan
  • Call Minutes
  • Charges
  • Customer Churn Status

Methodology

  • Cleaned missing and inconsistent data.
  • Validated calculations using SQL.
  • Created KPIs and DAX measures.
  • Designed an interactive Power BI dashboard.
  • Analysed customer behaviour across multiple dimensions.
  • Developed recommendations based on the findings.

Key Insights

  • Total Customers: 5,269
  • Churned Customers: 708
  • Retained Customers: 4,561
  • Overall Churn Rate: 13.44%
  • Churn varied across different states.
  • Customers with certain service plans showed different churn patterns.

Recommendations

  • Focus retention campaigns in high-churn regions.
  • Monitor customers showing early signs of churn.
  • Improve customer engagement programmes.
  • Review service offerings associated with higher churn.
