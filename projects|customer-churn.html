Customer Churn Analysis

SQL & Power BI analysis of customer retention and churn

Business Problem

A telecommunications business needs to understand customer churn and identify patterns that may be associated with customers leaving the business.

The objective of this project was to analyse customer data using SQL and Power BI to understand churn levels, customer retention and differences across area codes.

Business Questions

  • How many customers are in the dataset?
  • How many customers have churned?
  • How many customers have been retained?
  • What is the overall churn rate?
  • How does churn differ across area codes?
  • What customer characteristics are associated with churn?

Dataset

The dataset contains customer information including account length, area code, international and voicemail plans, usage activity, customer service calls and churn status.

Recommendations

  • Monitor customer service interactions to identify customers who may require additional support.
  • Compare churn performance across area codes and customer segments.
  • Identify customer behaviours associated with higher churn levels.
  • Use churn trends to support customer retention strategies.
