BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE PROJECT

Customer Churn Analysis

An end-to-end data analytics project analysing customer churn, customer behaviour, and factors associated with customer attrition.

Project Overview

This project analyses customer churn to identify factors contributing to customer attrition and provide recommendations for improving customer retention.

Business Problem

Customer churn directly impacts revenue and profitability. Understanding why customers leave enables businesses to develop effective retention strategies and improve customer relationships.

Objectives

  • Measure overall customer churn.
  • Identify regions with high churn.
  • Analyse customer behaviour.
  • Identify patterns associated with churn.
  • Recommend customer retention strategies.

Tools Used

Excel SQL Power BI DAX

Dataset Overview

The dataset contains customer information used to analyse customer behaviour and identify patterns associated with churn.

  • State
  • Area Code
  • Account Length
  • International Plan
  • Voice Mail Plan
  • Call Minutes
  • Charges
  • Customer Churn Status

Methodology

The project followed an end-to-end analytics workflow to clean, validate, analyse, and visualise customer churn data.

  1. Cleaned missing and inconsistent data.
  2. Validated calculations using SQL.
  3. Created KPIs and DAX measures.
  4. Designed an interactive Power BI dashboard.
  5. Analysed customer behaviour across multiple dimensions.
  6. Developed recommendations based on the findings.

SQL Analysis

SQL was used to clean, validate, and analyse the customer churn dataset before developing the Power BI dashboard.

Cleaned Data

The dataset was cleaned and validated to ensure the data used for analysis and reporting was accurate and consistent.

Power BI Dashboard

An interactive Power BI dashboard was developed to monitor customer churn, retention, and customer behaviour.

Overall Churn Rate

  • Churn varied across different states.
  • Customers with certain service plans showed different churn patterns.
  • Customers who contacted customer service more frequently tended to show higher churn, suggesting potential dissatisfaction or unresolved issues.

Recommendations

  • Focus retention campaigns in high-churn regions.
  • Monitor customers showing early signs of churn.
  • Improve customer engagement programmes.
  • Review service offerings associated with higher churn.
  • Investigate frequent customer service contacts as a potential early warning indicator.
