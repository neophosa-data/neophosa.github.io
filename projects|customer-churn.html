Customer Churn Analysis

Analysing customer churn, retention patterns, customer usage and service interactions using SQL and Power BI.

SQL Excel Power BI

THE BUSINESS PROBLEM

Understanding Customer Churn

Customer churn is an important business challenge because losing existing customers can reduce recurring revenue and increase the cost of acquiring replacements. Looking at individual customer records does not easily reveal the overall scale of churn or the patterns associated with customers leaving.

The objective of this project was to analyse customer data using SQL and Power BI to understand the size of the customer base, identify churn levels, and examine patterns that could help the business understand customer retention.

The analysis provides management with a clearer view of customer retention performance and highlights areas where targeted retention strategies could be considered.

BUSINESS INTERPRETATION

What the Data Tells Us

The analysis shows a customer base of 5,269 customers, of which 708 customers churned and 4,561 customers were retained.

This results in an overall churn rate of 13.44%, providing the business with a clear baseline for monitoring customer retention.

The analysis also shows an association between customer service calls and churn. Customers with more service interactions can therefore be an important segment to monitor when investigating retention.

Area code analysis also showed differences between geographic segments. Area code 415 contained 2,592 customers and 352 churned customers, making it an important segment to examine further.

Overall, the analysis shows that customer churn can be examined through customer service interactions, usage behaviour and geographic segments rather than relying on a single metric.
