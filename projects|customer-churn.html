TELECOM • CUSTOMER ANALYTICS

Customer Churn Analysis

Analysing customer retention, churn behaviour, service interactions and customer segments using SQL and Power BI.

01 • BUSINESS PROBLEM

Understanding Customer Churn

Customer churn reduces recurring revenue and increases the cost of acquiring new customers. Individual customer records make it difficult to understand the overall scale of churn and identify patterns across the customer base.

The objective of this project was to understand the customer base, churn levels and retention using SQL and Power BI.

The analysis provides management with a clearer view of customer behaviour and supports more targeted customer retention decisions.

06 • POWER BI DASHBOARD

Interactive Churn Dashboard

Power BI was used to transform the analysed customer data into an interactive dashboard for understanding churn, retention and customer behaviour.

Page 1 — Churn & Retention Overview

This page provides an overview of the customer base, churn rate, churned customers and retained customers.

It also provides visibility into geographic distribution and customer plan information, allowing overall churn performance to be explored interactively.

Page 2 — Customer Behaviour

This page focuses on customer behaviour by analysing customer service calls, voicemail usage, international usage and account length.

The dashboard allows differences between churned and retained customers to be explored and helps identify patterns associated with customer retention.

08 • BUSINESS INTERPRETATION

Understanding the Customer Base

Customer service interactions show an association with churn, with higher numbers of service calls providing an important area for further investigation.

Area code 415 contains 2,592 customers, of which 352 customers churned. This provides a useful segment for monitoring customer retention behaviour.

Overall, churn behaviour can be explored through customer service interactions, usage patterns and geographic segments.

09 • RECOMMENDATIONS

Business Recommendations

Investigate customer service experience

Analyse customers with frequent service calls to identify potential service issues contributing to churn.

Monitor higher-risk customer segments

Track churn across geographic and customer segments to identify areas requiring additional attention.

Identify customers at higher risk

Use customer behaviour and usage patterns to identify customers who may require retention interventions.

Monitor usage behaviour

Continue analysing international usage, voicemail activity and account characteristics alongside churn.

Build recurring churn monitoring

Use recurring reporting and dashboards to monitor changes in churn and retention over time.
