MY PROCESS

How I Approach Data Problems

A structured process from understanding the business problem to communicating the result.

01

Understand the problem

What does the business actually need to know?

02

Understand the data

03

Clean the data

Identify duplicates, missing values, inconsistent formats and other data-quality issues.

04

Analyze

Use SQL and Excel to explore data and calculate metrics that answer business questions.

05

Build

Create an interactive Power BI dashboard that makes the results easy to understand.

06

Explain

Explain the findings and what actions the data can support.

Goal: Don't just show the data. Explain what the data is saying.
