About Me

I’m a Data Analyst with a passion for transforming raw data into meaningful insights that support better business decisions.

I work across the analytics process, from cleaning and validating datasets to analysing trends, developing KPIs, building interactive Power BI dashboards, and generating actionable business insights.

My technical skills include Excel, SQL, Power BI, and DAX, which I use to develop end-to-end analytics projects across different business domains, including retail sales, customer churn, credit risk, and ATM transaction analysis.

I believe that data is most valuable when it tells a clear story. My goal is to help organisations make informed, data-driven decisions by delivering accurate analysis, intuitive dashboards, and practical business insights.

I’m continuously expanding my technical and analytical skills and look forward to contributing to organisations that value analytical thinking, problem-solving, and evidence-based decision-making.
