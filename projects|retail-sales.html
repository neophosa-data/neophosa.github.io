BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE PROJECT

Retail Sales Analysis

An end-to-end data analytics project analysing retail sales performance, customer purchasing behaviour, product performance, and sales trends.

Project Overview

This project analyses retail sales performance to identify sales trends, customer purchasing behaviour, and product performance. The goal was to transform raw sales data into meaningful insights that support better business decisions.

Business Problem

Retail businesses need to understand what drives sales and customer purchasing patterns. Without analysing sales data, it becomes difficult to identify high-performing products, seasonal trends, and opportunities to increase revenue.

Objectives

  • Analyse overall sales performance.
  • Identify monthly sales trends.
  • Evaluate product category performance.
  • Understand customer purchasing behaviour.
  • Provide recommendations to improve sales.

Tools Used

Excel SQL Power BI DAX

Dataset Overview

The dataset contains transactional retail sales data used to analyse sales performance and customer purchasing behaviour.

  • Order Date
  • Product Category
  • Quantity Sold
  • Sales Amount
  • Customer ID
  • Product Information

Methodology

The project followed an end-to-end analytics workflow, starting with data preparation and validation before moving into analysis and dashboard development.

  1. Cleaned and validated the dataset.
  2. Used SQL for data cleaning and validation.
  3. Created calculated measures and KPIs.
  4. Built an interactive Power BI dashboard.
  5. Analysed sales trends and business performance.
  6. Developed recommendations based on the findings.

SQL Analysis

SQL was used to clean, validate, and analyse the underlying retail sales data before developing the Power BI dashboard.

Cleaned Data

The dataset was cleaned and validated to ensure that the information used for analysis and reporting was accurate and consistent.

Power BI Dashboard

An interactive Power BI dashboard was developed to monitor key sales performance indicators and identify trends across the retail business.
