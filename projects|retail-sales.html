Retail Sales Analysis

Analysing sales performance, product categories, customer demographics and transaction behaviour using SQL and Power BI.

Business Problem

A retail business needs to understand sales across products, periods, and transactions. Individual records make it difficult to identify revenue categories, strongest and weakest periods, and average customer spend.

The objective of this project was to analyse retail sales using SQL and Power BI. SQL was used to clean, validate, and analyse the data, while Power BI was used to create an interactive dashboard.

The analysis supports revenue analysis, inventory planning, promotions, and understanding product performance.

Business Questions

The analysis focused on answering the following questions:

01

What are total sales and transaction volumes?

02

Which product categories generate most revenue?

03

How does sales performance change over time?

04

How much revenue per transaction?

05

Which areas could be improved?

Business Interpretation

Electronics generated the highest sales at approximately R156.9K, making it the strongest revenue-generating category.

Clothing recorded the highest quantity sold at 894 units, showing strong sales volume despite differences in revenue contribution across categories.

Female customers generated approximately R232.8K in sales compared with R223.2K from male customers.

Customers aged 46–55 generated approximately R100.7K in sales, making this an important customer age group within the dataset.

Overall sales performance was influenced by product category, customer demographics and sales volume.

Recommendations

  • Focus on strong-performing product categories while investigating opportunities to improve weaker categories.
  • Monitor sales volume alongside revenue to understand product demand.
  • Use customer demographic information to better understand important customer segments.
  • Monitor monthly sales trends to support planning, promotions and inventory decisions.
