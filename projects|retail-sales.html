SQL Excel Power BI

THE BUSINESS PROBLEM

Understanding Retail Sales Performance

A retail business needs to understand how its sales are performing across different products, periods, and transactions. Looking at individual sales records makes it difficult to identify which categories are generating the most revenue, when sales are strongest or weakest, and how much customers are spending per transaction.

The objective of this project was to use SQL and Power BI to transform raw retail sales data into useful business insights. SQL was used to clean, validate, and analyse the data, while Power BI was used to present the results through an interactive dashboard.

The analysis helps the business understand its sales performance and identify opportunities to improve revenue, inventory planning, promotions, and product performance.

BUSINESS INTERPRETATION

What the Data Tells Us

The analysis shows that the business generated R456,000 in total sales across 1,000 transactions, with 2,514 units sold.

Electronics generated the highest category sales at approximately R156.9K, making it the strongest category by revenue.

Clothing recorded the highest quantity sold, with 894 units, showing that a category can have strong sales volume even when it is not the highest revenue contributor.

Female customers generated approximately R232.8K in sales compared with R223.2K from male customers.

Customers aged 46–55 generated the highest sales among the age groups, contributing approximately R100.7K.

Overall, the analysis shows that sales performance is influenced by product category, customer demographics and sales volume.
