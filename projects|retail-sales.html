RETAIL • SALES ANALYTICS

Retail Sales Analysis

Analysing sales performance, product categories, customer demographics and transaction behaviour using SQL and Power BI.

01 • BUSINESS PROBLEM

Understanding Retail Sales Performance

A retail business needs to understand sales across products, periods, and transactions. Individual records make it difficult to identify revenue categories, strongest and weakest periods, and average customer spend.

The objective of this project was to analyse retail sales using SQL and Power BI. SQL was used to clean, validate, and analyse the data, while Power BI was used to create an interactive dashboard.

The analysis supports revenue analysis, inventory planning, promotions, and understanding product performance.

08 • BUSINESS INTERPRETATION

What the Analysis Shows

Electronics generated the highest sales at approximately R156.9K, making it the strongest-performing product category by revenue.

Clothing recorded the highest quantity sold at 894 units, showing strong product volume despite differences in revenue.

Female customers generated approximately R232.8K in sales compared with approximately R223.2K from male customers.

Customers aged 46–55 generated approximately R100.7K in sales, making this age group one of the strongest customer segments.

Overall sales performance was influenced by product category, customer demographics and sales volume.

09 • RECOMMENDATIONS

Business Recommendations

Focus on strong-performing categories

Monitor high-revenue categories and ensure inventory and promotional strategies support continued performance.

Monitor sales volume

Track quantity sold alongside revenue to identify products generating strong transaction volume.

Understand customer segments

Use demographic patterns to understand customer behaviour and support more targeted marketing.

Monitor sales trends

Continue tracking monthly performance to identify changes in demand and support planning decisions.
