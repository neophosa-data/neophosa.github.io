Business Problem

A retail business needs to understand its sales performance, product categories, customer segments and sales trends. Without analysing the available transaction data, it can be difficult to identify which products and customer groups are contributing most to revenue.

The objective of this project was to analyse retail transaction data using SQL and Power BI to identify sales patterns, customer characteristics and product performance.

SQL was used to clean, validate and analyse the data before the results were presented through an interactive Power BI dashboard.

Business Questions

  • What are the total sales and number of transactions?
  • Which product categories generate the most sales?
  • How many units are being sold?
  • What is the average sale per transaction?
  • How do sales change over time?
  • Which customer segments contribute most to sales?

Recommendations

  • Focus on strong-performing product categories and identify opportunities to increase their contribution.
  • Monitor sales volumes alongside revenue to understand changes in purchasing behaviour.
  • Analyse customer segments to better understand which groups contribute most to sales.
  • Monitor sales trends over time to support inventory and sales planning.
