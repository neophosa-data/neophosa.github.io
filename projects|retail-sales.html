1. Retail Sales Analysis

Project Overview

This project analyses retail sales performance to identify sales trends, customer purchasing behaviour, and product performance. The goal was to transform raw sales data into meaningful insights that support better business decisions.

Business Problem

Retail businesses need to understand what drives sales and customer purchasing patterns. Without analysing sales data, it becomes difficult to identify high-performing products, seasonal trends, and opportunities to increase revenue.

Objectives

  • Analyse overall sales performance.
  • Identify monthly sales trends.
  • Evaluate product category performance.
  • Understand customer purchasing behaviour.
  • Provide recommendations to improve sales.

Tools Used

  • Excel
  • SQL
  • Power BI
  • DAX

Dataset Overview

The dataset contains transactional retail sales data, including:

  • Order Date
  • Product Category
  • Quantity Sold
  • Sales Amount
  • Customer ID
  • Product Information

Methodology

  • Cleaned and validated the dataset.
  • Used SQL for data cleaning and validation.
  • Created calculated measures and KPIs.
  • Built an interactive Power BI dashboard.
  • Analysed sales trends and business performance.
  • Developed recommendations based on the findings.

  • Sales varied across months, indicating seasonal purchasing patterns.
  • Certain product categories generated higher revenue than others.

Recommendations

  • Increase inventory for high-performing products.
  • Run promotions during slower sales periods.
  • Focus marketing on top-selling categories.
  • Monitor sales trends regularly.
