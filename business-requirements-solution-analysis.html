BUSINESS REQUIREMENTS & SOLUTION ANALYSIS

Requirements Analysis & Solution Design

Requirements Gathering · Stakeholder Analysis · Functional Requirements · Process Analysis · Solution Design

Business Problem

The business required a structured way to translate stakeholder needs into clear and actionable solution requirements.

Without clearly documented requirements, there is a risk of scope creep, misunderstood stakeholder expectations and solutions that do not address the original business problem.

Objective

Analyse stakeholder needs and translate them into clearly defined business, functional and reporting requirements.

Stakeholder Analysis

Key stakeholders were identified based on their involvement in the process and their information needs.

Stakeholder groups:

  • Business management
  • Operational users
  • Analysts
  • Process owners
  • Technical/solution teams
Stakeholder Map

Requirements

Business Requirements

  • Improve process visibility
  • Improve information quality
  • Support better decision-making
  • Reduce process inefficiencies

Functional Requirements

  • Capture required business information
  • Allow users to access relevant information
  • Support process monitoring
  • Enable reporting against defined KPIs

Reporting Requirements

  • KPI monitoring
  • Trend analysis
  • Filtering by relevant business dimensions
  • Management-level reporting
Requirements Documentation

Process Analysis

I analysed the process to understand where requirements were being met and where improvements were required.

Process Flow

Solution Recommendation

The proposed solution aligned:

Stakeholder Needs → Business Requirements → Process → Data → Reporting

What I Learned

The project strengthened my ability to convert stakeholder needs into clear requirements that can be understood by both business and technical teams.
