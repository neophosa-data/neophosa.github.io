ATM Withdrawal Analysis

SQL & Power BI analysis of ATM withdrawal activity and transaction patterns

Business Problem

A banking business needs to understand ATM withdrawal activity, transaction volumes, withdrawal values and customer usage patterns.

The objective of this project was to analyse ATM withdrawal data using SQL and Power BI to identify transaction trends, card usage and withdrawal behaviour.

Business Questions

  • How many ATM withdrawals were made?
  • What is the total amount withdrawn?
  • What is the average withdrawal amount?
  • How do withdrawals change over time?
  • How do Mastercard and Visa transactions compare?
  • Which locations record the highest withdrawal activity?

Dataset

The dataset contains ATM transaction information including withdrawal amounts, transaction dates, ATM locations and card types.

Data Cleaning & Preparation

The raw ATM withdrawal data was reviewed and prepared using SQL before calculating the main metrics and developing the Power BI dashboard.

Recommendations

  • Monitor ATM withdrawal trends to support cash availability and operational planning.
  • Compare transaction activity across locations to identify areas with higher ATM usage.
  • Monitor card-type transaction patterns to understand customer payment behaviour.
  • Track withdrawal values and volumes together when assessing ATM performance.
