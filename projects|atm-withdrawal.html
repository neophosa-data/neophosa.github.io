THE BUSINESS PROBLEM

Understanding ATM Transaction Behaviour

A financial institution processes a large number of ATM transactions every day. Without analysing these transactions, it can be difficult to understand withdrawal behaviour, transaction volumes, withdrawal values, and differences between payment networks such as Mastercard and Visa.

The objective of this project was to analyse ATM withdrawal transactions using SQL and Power BI. SQL was used to clean, validate, and analyse the transaction data, while Power BI was used to identify trends and present the findings through an interactive dashboard.

The analysis provides insight into ATM usage and transaction behaviour, helping the business understand demand and identify opportunities to improve ATM operations and customer service.

BUSINESS INTERPRETATION

What the Data Tells Us

The analysis shows a high volume of ATM activity, with 1,429,400 withdrawals recorded across the dataset.

The total amount withdrawn was approximately R6.05 billion, highlighting the scale of transaction value processed through the ATM network.

The calculated average withdrawal amount was R522,305.88 based on the transaction measure used in the analysis.

Mastercard represented approximately 54.78% of the card activity, allowing the business to compare Mastercard and Visa usage.

Withdrawal activity also differed between weekdays and weekends, with approximately 893,391 weekday withdrawals compared with 536,009 weekend withdrawals.

Location-level analysis also showed differences in ATM activity, with KK Nagar standing out as a high-activity location in the dataset.
