ATM Withdrawal Analysis

Analysing ATM withdrawal activity, transaction values, card networks, locations and withdrawal patterns using SQL and Power BI.

← Back to Projects

Business Problem

A financial institution processes a large number of ATM transactions every day. Without analysing these transactions, it can be difficult to understand withdrawal behaviour, transaction volumes, withdrawal values, and differences between payment networks such as Mastercard and Visa.

The objective of this project was to analyse ATM withdrawal transactions using SQL and Power BI. SQL was used to clean, validate, and analyse the transaction data, while Power BI was used to identify trends and present the findings through an interactive dashboard.

The analysis provides insight into ATM usage and transaction behaviour, helping the business understand demand and identify opportunities to improve ATM operations and customer service.

Business Questions

  1. How many ATM withdrawals were processed?
  2. What was the total amount withdrawn?
  3. What was the average withdrawal amount?
  4. How did withdrawal activity change over time?
  5. How did Mastercard and Visa withdrawal activity compare?

Business Interpretation

The dataset contains 1,429,400 withdrawals with a total withdrawal value of approximately R6.05 billion. The calculated average withdrawal amount was R522,305.88 based on the transaction measure used in the analysis.

Mastercard represented 54.78% of withdrawal activity. Withdrawal activity was also higher on weekdays, with 893,391 withdrawals compared with 536,009 on weekends.

ATM location and time-based patterns can help identify areas with higher transaction activity and support better monitoring of ATM demand.

Recommendations

  • Monitor ATM transaction volumes to identify periods of high demand.
  • Use withdrawal patterns to support ATM operational planning.
  • Compare Mastercard and Visa activity when monitoring transaction behaviour.
  • Monitor high-activity ATM locations to support better resource planning.
  • Establish recurring transaction reporting to monitor changes in ATM usage over time.
