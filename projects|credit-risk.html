Credit Risk Analysis

Using SQL and Power BI to understand loan exposure, borrower characteristics and credit risk.

01 — BUSINESS PROBLEM

Business Problem

A financial institution needs to understand the level of risk it takes when providing loans. Analysing thousands of loan applications individually makes it difficult to identify patterns in defaults, borrower characteristics, loan amounts, and interest rates.


The objective of this project was to analyse loan application data using SQL and Power BI to identify patterns associated with credit risk and loan defaults. SQL was used to clean, validate, and analyse the data before presenting the findings in an interactive Power BI dashboard.


The analysis gives the business a clearer view of its loan portfolio and helps identify areas where lending decisions and risk management could potentially be improved.

What I Wanted to Find Out

  • What is the overall loan default rate?
  • How much money has the institution lent?
  • What is the average loan amount?
  • What is the average applicant income?
  • What patterns can be identified among borrowers and loan risk?

What the Data Tells Us

The data tells the story of how borrower characteristics and loan attributes can be used to understand credit risk.


The dataset contains 28,638 loan applications, with a total loan amount of approximately R276.5 million. The loan status variable provides an important measure for distinguishing between different loan outcomes and assessing the level of credit risk within the portfolio.


The analysis looks at factors such as income, employment length, loan amount, interest rate, loan grade, loan purpose, home ownership, credit history, and previous defaults.


Loan grade provides an important indication of borrower risk, while loan-to-income levels help show how significant a loan may be relative to a person's income. Previous credit defaults and the length of a borrower's credit history also provide additional information that can help assess creditworthiness.


Overall, the numbers tell a story of different levels of financial risk across borrowers. Analysing these characteristics allows a business to better understand which borrower profiles may represent higher or lower credit risk.

07 — RECOMMENDATIONS

Recommendations

  • Strengthen credit assessment for applicants showing higher-risk characteristics.
  • Monitor default rates regularly across borrower segments.
  • Compare applicant income against requested loan amounts when assessing affordability.
  • Analyse default patterns to improve future lending decisions.
  • Use portfolio-level risk monitoring to identify increases in credit exposure early.
