Business Problem

A lending business needs to understand its loan portfolio, default levels, loan exposure and applicant characteristics. Analysing these measures can help identify areas of higher credit risk and support better lending decisions.

The objective of this project was to analyse credit risk data using SQL and Power BI and present the results through an interactive dashboard.

Business Questions

  • What is the overall default rate?
  • What is the total loan exposure?
  • What is the average loan amount?
  • How does applicant income compare across the portfolio?
  • What is the average interest rate?
  • How is credit risk distributed across the loan portfolio?

Dataset

The dataset contains information relating to loan applications, applicant characteristics, loan amounts, interest rates and loan status.

Data Cleaning & Preparation

The credit risk dataset was reviewed and prepared using SQL before the analysis and Power BI visualisation.

Recommendations

  • Track total loan exposure to identify areas where credit risk may be concentrated.
  • Use applicant and loan characteristics to support more informed credit decisions.
  • Continue monitoring portfolio performance over time.
