Credit Risk Analysis

Analysing borrower characteristics, loan exposure, interest rates and default risk using SQL and Power BI.

Business Problem

A financial institution needs to understand the factors influencing loan defaults and overall credit risk. Without structured analysis, it can be difficult to identify patterns across borrower characteristics, loan amounts, interest rates and default behaviour.

The objective of this project was to clean and analyse the credit risk dataset using SQL and Power BI to provide a clearer view of the loan portfolio and borrower risk.

Business Questions

The analysis focused on answering the following questions:

01

What is the overall loan default rate?

02

How much money has the institution lent?

03

What is the average loan amount?

04

What is the average applicant income?

05

What patterns can be identified among borrowers and loan risk?

Dataset

The dataset contains borrower, loan and credit-history information used to analyse lending behaviour and default risk.

SQL Analysis

SQL was used to analyse the cleaned dataset and calculate key credit-risk and portfolio metrics.

01

Overall Default Rate

The overall default rate was calculated to understand the proportion of loan applications that resulted in default.

21.66% Default Rate

Approximately one in five applications defaulted, highlighting the level of credit risk within the portfolio.

02

Total Loan Exposure

The total loan amount was calculated to understand the institution's overall exposure across the analysed portfolio.

R276.5M Total Loan Exposure

The portfolio represents significant financial exposure, making effective credit-risk monitoring important.

03

Average Loan Amount

The average loan amount was calculated to understand the typical size of loans within the portfolio.

R9,656 Average Loan Amount
04

Applicant Income

Average applicant income was analysed to understand the income profile of borrowers in the dataset.

R66,649 Average Applicant Income
05

Average Interest Rate

The average loan interest rate was calculated to understand the pricing of credit across the portfolio.

11.04% Average Interest Rate

Power BI Dashboard

Power BI was used to transform the analysis into an interactive dashboard covering portfolio performance, borrower risk and borrower segments.

Page 1 — Portfolio Overview

This page provides an overview of loan applications, loan amounts, interest rates, default status, loan grades and home ownership. It provides a high-level view of the overall loan portfolio and its risk profile.

Page 2 — Borrower Risk

This page analyses borrower age groups, income, employment length and loan grades. It helps identify differences in borrower characteristics and how they relate to loan status and risk.

Page 3 — Borrower Segments

This page explores loan purposes, home ownership, credit history and age groups to identify different borrower segments within the portfolio.

Recommendations

  • Strengthen credit assessment processes for higher-risk applicants.
  • Monitor default rates across different borrower segments.
  • Compare applicant income with requested loan amounts when assessing affordability and risk.
  • Analyse recurring default patterns to identify higher-risk borrower characteristics.
  • Use portfolio-level dashboards for ongoing credit-risk monitoring and decision-making.
