BANKING • CREDIT RISK

Credit Risk Analysis

Analysing borrower characteristics, loan exposure, interest rates and default risk using SQL and Power BI.

SQL Excel Power BI

THE BUSINESS PROBLEM

Understanding Loan Portfolio Risk

A financial institution needs to understand the level of risk within its loan portfolio. Without analysing borrower information and loan characteristics, it can be difficult to identify patterns in defaults, borrower characteristics, loan amounts and interest rates.

The objective of this project was to clean and analyse loan data using SQL and Power BI. The analysis focused on understanding default behaviour, borrower characteristics and overall loan exposure.

The resulting analysis provides a clearer view of the loan portfolio and helps identify areas that could support stronger credit risk monitoring.

POWER BI

Building the Dashboard

After analysing the loan data in SQL, I used Power BI to create an interactive dashboard covering portfolio performance, borrower risk and borrower segments.

DASHBOARD PAGE 01

Portfolio Overview

This page provides an overview of loan applications, loan amounts, interest rates, default status, loan grades and home ownership.

It provides a high-level view of the overall loan portfolio and allows users to understand the scale and risk profile of the lending activity.

DASHBOARD PAGE 02

Borrower Risk

This page analyses borrower age groups, income, employment length, loan grade and borrower characteristics.

The purpose is to identify differences between borrower groups and understand how borrower characteristics relate to credit risk.

DASHBOARD PAGE 03

Borrower Segments

This page examines loan purposes, home ownership, credit history and age groups to provide additional borrower segmentation.

These views help provide additional context around the characteristics of borrowers within the portfolio and their potential risk profiles.
