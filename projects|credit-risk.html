Business Problem

A financial institution needs to understand the level of risk it takes when providing loans. Analysing thousands of loan applications individually makes it difficult to identify patterns in defaults, borrower characteristics, loan amounts, and interest rates.

The objective of this project was to analyse loan application data using SQL and Power BI to identify patterns associated with credit risk and loan defaults.

SQL was used to clean, validate, and analyse the data before presenting the findings in an interactive Power BI dashboard.

The analysis gives the business a clearer view of its loan portfolio and helps identify areas where lending decisions and risk management could potentially be improved.

Business Questions

  • What is the overall loan default rate?
  • What is the total loan exposure?
  • What is the average loan amount?
  • What is the average applicant income?
  • What is the average interest rate?
  • What patterns can be identified within the loan portfolio?

Recommendations

  • Strengthen credit assessment for higher-risk applicants.
  • Monitor default rates across different borrower segments.
  • Compare applicant income with requested loan amounts to support affordability assessment.
  • Analyse default patterns regularly to identify changes in portfolio risk.
  • Use portfolio-level monitoring to support better lending and risk-management decisions.
