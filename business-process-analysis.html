BUSINESS PROCESS ANALYSIS

Business Process Mapping & Data Modelling

Requirements Analysis · Process Mapping · Gap Analysis · Data Modelling · Microsoft visio

Business Problem

The organisation needed a clearer understanding of its business process, the activities involved, the stakeholders responsible for each stage, and the information required to support the process.

The lack of a structured view made it difficult to identify process gaps and understand how business activities connected to the underlying data.

Objective

Act as a Business Analyst to analyse the existing process, identify business requirements, document process flows, identify gaps and create a structured data model.

Business Requirements

The analysis focused on:

  • Understanding stakeholder needs
  • Defining the project scope
  • Identifying key business activities
  • Identifying process inputs and outputs
  • Identifying business rules
  • Defining information requirements
  • Establishing relationships between business entities

Current-State Process

I mapped the existing workflow using Microsoft Visio to visualise:

  • Process activities
  • Decision points
  • Stakeholders
  • Inputs
  • Outputs
  • Process dependencies
Current-State Process Map

Gap Analysis

I evaluated the current process to identify areas where business requirements were not being fully addressed.

Area Current State Identified Gap Improvement
Process Multiple activities Limited visibility Standardised workflow
Data Information spread across entities Poor structure Structured data model
Reporting Limited visibility Difficult KPI tracking Defined reporting requirements
Ownership Roles not clearly represented Process ambiguity Clear responsibility
Gap Analysis

Future-State Process

I designed a future-state process to improve information flow, clarify responsibilities and better align the workflow with business requirements.

Future-State Process

Data Model

I identified the major business entities and their relationships.

Data Model

Key BA Deliverables

Requirements → Process Map → Gap Analysis → Future-State Process → Data Model

Recommendations

  • Standardise the process
  • Clearly define process ownership
  • Improve data capture
  • Establish consistent business rules
  • Structure data around key business entities
  • Define reporting requirements before solution development

What I Learned

This project demonstrated how a Business Analyst moves from understanding the business problem to defining requirements, modelling processes and translating business needs into a structured solution.
